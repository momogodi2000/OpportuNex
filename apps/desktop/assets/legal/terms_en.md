## 4. Opportunity information
Opportunities come from third-party public sources. Opportunex:
- shows the source and official link of every opportunity;
- flags inferred or generated information, and shows "Not specified" when information is missing;
- does not guarantee that listings are accurate, up to date or still open;
- never states that you are eligible: always check the conditions with the publisher.

You apply only through the publisher's official channel. Opportunex never submits an application on your behalf.
